I dreamt of a world with a vast coastline. Black tendrils broke the surface of the sand and the waves, and a great white star beamed down on me from the horizon. Black stars dotted the tenebrous skies and I felt a cool mist wash over me. I saw distant figures on all horizons and ran to catch the northern one. My legs gave out and I fell into the waters. Water filled my lungs as the inky depths drank life from me and I struggled for precious air. Everything faded to eigengrau mist and  when I awoke, I was left with nothing but an overwhelming sense of peace.
