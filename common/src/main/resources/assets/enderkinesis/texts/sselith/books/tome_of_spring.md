A: The circle is drawn, the candles are lit, let the record show that we convene to discuss Rebounding, and we shall do so in the proper and dignified manner most befitting the University of Ssel.
G: So recorded.
A: You performed the rite yesterday as instructed, Gallavan?
G: I did. Upon the Membrane most Taut, beneath the golden light, as prescribed in the Codex.
A: The Membrane most Taut being...?
G: The circular altar of cloth, suspended over the void by 34 and 5 coiled springs of binding.
A: I believe it simply to be named "a trampoline".
G: Anatas, we are in the Annotations.
A: Nevertheless, simply referring to mundane objects as they are named by anyone else suffices for our records.
G: It may be mundane, but in the Light most Holy it is consecrated, and should be referred to with respect.

A: Very well. Begin description of the ascent.
G: I began, as one must, with Actions Little. The low oscillations by which the body begins to trust the Membrane most Taut. Then, the Actions Middle. The springs begin to resonate in tune with the body.
A: Go on.
G: The final act is the Great Rising. At the apex of the seventh bound, there was a moment. A fraction of a second, where I was neither ascending, nor descending.
A: Did you slip between parallels?
G: No, I don't believe I was able to reach high enough.
