On the Cultivation and Behavior of the Sculk
A Preliminary Report to the Council of Resonance, Third Course
Submitted by Valen Softcallous, Junior Archivist, Annotations

Abstract
This report describes the Sculk, of which our colleagues of the City have been cultivating with admirable persistence. This Sculk appears to be a living network that responds to auditory stimulus. It feeds off of souls and communicates through sought-after Resonance.

1. Introduction

When I came to the City, the Sculk was a scientific curiosity. Dark blue growths spotted in the slate structuring of the city, pulsing in a remarkable manner. Most of the City determined it to be some form of fungus, lichen, or unusual mineral.

We of the University of Ssel of course knew better. Master Sereth was the first to notice the nature of the organism to respond to noise. Debates shot up among the populace of the City as to the nature of the organism, claiming ferociously that it was utterly harmless and could not possibly be listening.

It seems that, whether by some mind-altering phenomenon, some Ulder deity, or simply an attempt at deception, the people of the City refuse to acknowledge the dangers of the Sculk. Perhaps they are cultivating it, to some unknown end.

2. Morphology

Over several seasons I have studied the progression of the Sculk organism. While the Sculk itself is divided into individual cells like any bacterium or fungal spore, they form into distinct structures that can be identified and Cataloged Most Holy.
Sculk Mass - Basic tissue of the body. Soft texture, slimy, and contains soul essences. Can be harvested at risk of disturbing the body for the magical energy digested within.
Sculk Veins - This appears to be how the Sculk spreads. Thin filaments that run along walls and ceilings.
Sculk Sensor - This is a remarkable structure. Primarily composed of the mass. Tendrils grow from the mass and ride ambient resonant waves. When sufficient resonance is gathered, the being seems to digest the resonance into electrical signals. More study is requested as to the nature of if it is purely communicative or has some ulterior cause.
Sculk Catalyst - This is the primary storage point and resonator for Soul Energy. The catalyst acts as the nervous system for a Sculk macroorganism, and allows it to gather ambient soul energy to grow.
