## Page I — Of the Carrying

The carrying is the breath made small enough to bear an item from one orb to another. The send orb pulls from the vessel that touches its active face; the receive orb deposits into the vessel that touches its own. Place the orbs against chests, against barrels, against any container that will yield or accept.

## Page II — The Pairing

Aim the tome at the orb that shall send. Aim then at the orb that shall receive. The thread is set, and the carrying begins at the next tick. Each tick passes one stack from the send to one receiver, and the journey takes one tick for each pace of distance.

## Page III — Of the Many Receivers

A single send may serve many receivers. The stacks are distributed in turn, each receiver taking the next in rotation. None is favoured. The send does not weary, the rotation does not falter, and over time the receivers share the load equally.

## Page IV — Of the Ghost Stack

When a stack is dispatched the reader may see it cross the air, an apparition of the item flying its short journey, trailing the purple breath of Sselith. Touch it not — it will not be picked up, for the breath holds it apart from common things until the carrying is done.

## Page V — Of Spillage

If the receiver's vessel is full or absent, the stack does not vanish. It falls instead from the receive orb's air-side, dropped as common items are dropped, for the reader to gather. No breath is wasted; no stack is ever lost to the carrying.

## Page VI — On Severance

To halt a carrying, aim the tome at the pair once more and the link is undone. Any stacks in flight to that receiver will fall at the receiver's place; nothing is destroyed. To halt every carrying through an orb, sneak and click — and stand ready to gather what falls.
