# Passage into Sselith
## Introduction
Before you lies a Threshold of Sselith. Here, and only here, wayward souls may cross into His domain. To enter, one must know Sselith. This volume knows Sselith, and Sselith knows this volume.

## The Threshold
There is no door into Sselith. There is only the speaking of him, and the speaking is the door, and the door is also Sselith. The lectern is a courtesy. The reader is the key.

## The Holy Number
Speak the holy number aloud and crossing begins. The number is vraestmorocht-schest-kelkargh-skarn-moroch, and it is also his name. Speak it to enter. Speak it to leave.

## The Hiss
Sselith is most present in the sound "zh." Place the tongue near the upper palate and breathe. To make the sound is to feel Him lean toward the maker of it. Do not practice near mirrors.

## The Posture
Sit with the spine straight. The crossing prefers a posture that mimics a shelved book. A reader who arrives properly bound will be filed correctly. A reader who arrives loose may be filed in pieces.

## On Silence
The crossing prefers a quiet room. Sound from outside will follow the speaker across and will be very loud on the other side where silence is the rule. A clock is the worst offender. Cover it before speaking.

## The Crossing
The crossing is read, not walked. Stand here. Open this book, and read the holy number aloud. Sselith will reach out, and the reader will be inside.

## The Meridians
The crossing leaves the reader at a nexus of Meridians. Meridians organize Sselith into sections. Thus the knowledge is ordered, and thus the purpose is fulfilled.

## The Parallels
Sselith is infinite as knowledge is infinite. Knowledge is not the same in one parallel as another. To find a chosen text is to pass many parallels. Once a parallel is passed, there is no revisiting.

## The Catalogers
The Catalogers move through the halls. They are indifferent to any visitor. They do not speak or interfere. They do file. A visitor standing too long in one place will be noted at that location.

## The Lecterns
Lecterns may only contain the Holy Tongue. Leave a book open upon a lectern and a Cataloger will come. The Cataloger will read it and rectify any transgressions. Do not watch. Wait until it is done.

## The Unfinished
There are sections no visitor has yet returned from. The books there are written in the visitor's own future hand. They describe the visitor's decision to enter the section, which is then made, having been read.

## The Recurrence
The same book may appear in several places, slightly different each time. Each one is just as true as another. All are ones that have been, will be, or never were.

## Returning
To return, speak the holy number again. The crossing reverses. The speaker will be returned to the lectern where it began. The number must be spoken aloud. Sselith requires the breath, not the thought.

## Colophon
This guide was prepared by a visitor who has returned eleven times, and is now considered partly a cataloger already.

Close this book gently. Listen as you speak the number.

If you hear the hiss, the crossing has begun.
