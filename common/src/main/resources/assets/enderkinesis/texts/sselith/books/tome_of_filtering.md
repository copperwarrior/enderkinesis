The name, I am told, comes from the sound the ritual makes. Onomatopoeic in nature. So ancient and self-evident is this practice that it requires no name, just an unearthly echo of its true behavior.

Before you stands the Temple. Within the temple, one thousand and one columns support the building. You must fall from one to the other, propel yourself from the ever-resonance of the crystalline marble stone.

Row upon row upon row of upright cabinets holding glasses of frothing lacquer. Gases venting from them speak holy scripture to some dead god of chance.

At the end (bottom?) lie seven doors. One door leads to fortune, six and one lead to a dark fate. May you receive what comes to you.
